Will there be ECS bounce charges or any penalties if I skip an installment?

No, there will be no charges or penalties if you skip an SIP installment. You can skip an SIP installment without worrying about ECS bounce charges, late fees, or any other penalties.

Your SIP will simply resume from the next scheduled installment date without impacting your existing investments, future returns, or the overall performance of your SIP portfolio. It’s a hassle-free process, ensuring your investments remain secure.
