What amount was charged for my order?

To check the charges for your order, follow these steps:

- Visit the Pricing Page: Click the button below to view detailed pricing information on Groww.

- Check Contract Note: At the end of each trading day, you'll receive a contract note by email with all applicable charges. You can also download it from the 'Reports' section.

- Preview Charges Before Order: On the order card, check the approximate charges by looking at the dotted line below the required margin before placing the order.
