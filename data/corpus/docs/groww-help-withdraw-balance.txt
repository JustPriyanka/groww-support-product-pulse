How do I withdraw money from my Groww account?

To withdraw money from your Groww account, follow these steps:

- Go to the 'Profile' section.

- Tap on 'Stocks, F&O balance'.

- Tap on 'Withdraw'.

- Enter the amount you want to withdraw.

- Click 'Withdraw' to complete your request.

If you have more than one bank account registered with Groww, choose the bank account for the withdrawal on the withdrawal screen.

Note: It may take up to 1 business day for the money to reflect in your bank account. Instant withdrawals are available from 9:30 AM to 6 PM if the amount is under 2 lakh. If it's above 2 lakh, it will take up to 24 hours.
