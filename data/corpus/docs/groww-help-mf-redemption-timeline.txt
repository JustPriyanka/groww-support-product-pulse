When will I get the redeemed amount?

The time it takes to receive your redeemed amount depends on the type of mutual fund:

- Liquid Funds: You'll get your redemption on the next working day (T+1).

- Other Domestic Mutual Funds (Equity, Debt, Hybrid, etc.): It takes about 3 working days (T+3) for the redemption to process.

- Overseas/International Mutual Funds: Due to cross-border settlement timelines, it may take 6 to 7 working days (T+6 to T+7).
