When are DP charges levied?

DP charges apply when a scrip is debited from your demat account. These charges are calculated per sell transaction.

Here's a breakdown of the charges:

- Depository Charges:

- ₹3.5 per sell transaction

- ₹3.25 per sell transaction for women

- Groww Charges: ₹16.5 per sell transaction(unless the sell value is under ₹100, then ₹0)

- GST: An 18% GST applies to the total of Depository and Groww charges.
