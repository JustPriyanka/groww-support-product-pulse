How does MTF work?

MTF allows you to buy stocks by paying only a portion of the total cost (25%) upfront, while Groww funds the remaining amount as a loan.

How to place an MTF order?

Switch to the MTF option

While buying a stock, switch to the “MTF" tab instead of the "Delivery" tab. The required amount will adjust based on the stock's margin.

Place your order

If you have the required Groww balance, your MTF order will be placed. Congratulations on completing your first order!

Monitor your MTF positions

View your MTF positions under the "Positions" tab next to "Explore" tab. Track and exit your positions all in one place.
